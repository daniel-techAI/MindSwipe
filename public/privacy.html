Privacy Policy

MindSwipe is built to work without an account, advertising profile, analytics SDK, remote user database, or location tracking. This policy explains the small number of situations where information can leave your device.

Effective and last updated: September 2, 2026

No accountNo MindSwipe login or cloud profile.
Local progressLearning history and trips stay on this device.
No tracking SDKNo analytics, ads, fingerprinting, or sale of data.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to the MindSwipe web app, progressive web app, and Android app. MindSwipe is operated by Daniel Laky, an independent developer. For privacy or support questions, email daniellaky5.c@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

MindSwipe stores the following in local browser or app storage so the product can work and remember your choices:

  • tutorial and onboarding status, selected interests, content preferences, and reminder settings;
  • completed, recent, and saved card IDs, saved place IDs, XP, streaks, and session counters;
  • trips, day and stop organization, travel mode, and notes you write;
  • a local migration backup or corrupt-record backup when needed to protect progress during an update.

This information is processed on your device. MindSwipe does not transmit it to a MindSwipe server. Android cloud backup is disabled.

4. Hosting and support providers

The public web app and policy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request and security data under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Support email is received through Google Gmail, which processes email under the Google Privacy Policy.

Support correspondence is used only to respond, investigate issues, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. It is normally deleted within 12 months after the matter is closed unless a longer period is reasonably needed for security, dispute, or legal records.

5. Notifications and device permissions

Reminders are optional and scheduled locally. Android may request notification permission. If you choose Background exact, Android may also ask you to allow Alarms and reminders access. Flexible reminders do not request exact timing access.

The Android app declares internet, notification, and exact-alarm permissions. It does not declare precise, approximate, foreground, or background location permission.

6. Purposes and legal bases

On-device information is used to provide the app features you request. Where data protection law applies to support communications, the operator processes them to answer your request, perform requested support, maintain security, and pursue legitimate interests in correcting and protecting MindSwipe. Information may also be retained where required by law or needed to establish or defend legal claims.

7. Sharing, advertising, and automated decisions

MindSwipe does not sell personal data. It does not share local progress with advertisers or analytics providers. It contains no advertising SDK, marketing SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, fingerprinting, or automated decision system that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Information may be disclosed if required by valid law, necessary to protect users or the service, or involved in a future transfer of the project. Where legally required, users will be informed before materially different processing begins.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you reset it, clear browser/app storage, or uninstall MindSwipe. Use Profile - Settings - Reset local progress to remove the active local record and migration or recovery backups. Because MindSwipe has no account or server copy, there is no remote account record to delete.

Deleting local data cannot remove information you independently sent to an external service. Contact that service or email MindSwipe support for support correspondence.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal data processed by the operator, and to complain to a data protection authority. Most MindSwipe data is only on your device and can be viewed or deleted there. For support records or another privacy request, email the address below. Identity may need to be reasonably verified before fulfilling a request.

10. Age

MindSwipe is intended for people aged 16 and older and is not designed as a child-directed service. If you believe a person under 16 has sent personal information through support, contact the operator so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

11. Security and policy changes

MindSwipe limits data collection, uses HTTPS for hosted pages, disables Android cleartext traffic and cloud backup, and keeps signing credentials outside the source repository. No method is risk-free, so do not place passwords, payment information, medical information, or other highly sensitive data in trip notes or support messages.

If accounts, cloud sync, analytics, ads, payments, AI services, remote packs, crash reporting, or location are introduced, this policy and store disclosures must be updated before those features are released. Material changes will receive a new effective date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
