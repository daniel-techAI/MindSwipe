Contact support

Email daniellaky5.c@gmail.com. Include what happened, what you expected, whether you use Android/PWA/web, your device or browser version, reproducible steps, and a screenshot only if it contains no sensitive information.

Do not send passwords, payment information, identity documents, medical records, or private trip details.

Delete local MindSwipe data

  1. Open MindSwipe.
  2. Choose Profile, then Settings.
  3. Choose Reset local progress and confirm.

You can also clear site/app storage or uninstall. MindSwipe has no server account or cloud copy to delete. Support emails and public GitHub posts are separate; email support if you need help with a support record.

Notification help

Background reminders require the Android app and notification permission. Background flexible may arrive near the selected time. Background exact may require Android Alarms and reminders access. Battery optimization and manufacturer settings can still affect delivery.

Turn reminders off under Profile - Quote reminder, or use Android notification settings.

Maps and route help

MindSwipe hands selected destinations or route segments to Google Maps or Waze. The external service completes navigation. Long trips are split so stops are not silently removed. If a place lacks enough verified location data, navigation remains unavailable.

Content corrections and rights concerns

Email the card/place name, exact issue, reliable source, and proposed correction. For image, copyright, trademark, or attribution concerns, identify the material and explain your relationship to the rights. Good-faith reports will be reviewed and material corrected or removed where appropriate.

Security reports

Do not post an exploitable vulnerability publicly. Email with the subject MindSwipe security report, describe the affected version, reproduction steps, and potential impact, and allow reasonable investigation time before disclosure.

Public issue tracker

Non-sensitive bugs and feature requests can be posted to MindSwipe GitHub Issues. Issues are public. Never include personal data, security secrets, private travel plans, or content you cannot publish.
