# MindSwipe Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 2, 2026

Public version: https://daniel-techai.github.io/MindSwipe/privacy.html

MindSwipe is built to work without an account, advertising profile, analytics SDK, remote user database, or location tracking. This policy applies to the MindSwipe web app, progressive web app, and Android app.

## 1. Operator and contact

MindSwipe is operated by Daniel Laky, an independent developer. Privacy and support email: `daniellaky5.c@gmail.com`.

## 2. Information stored on the device

MindSwipe stores tutorial and onboarding status, interests, preferences, reminder settings, completed/recent/saved card IDs, saved place IDs, XP, streaks, session counters, trips, day and stop organization, travel mode, and user-written notes in local browser or app storage. A local migration or corrupt-record backup can be kept to protect progress during an update.

This information is processed on the device and is not transmitted to a MindSwipe server. Android cloud backup is disabled.

## 3. When information can leave the device

- Loading the hosted web app sends standard request information, such as IP address, browser details, and request time, to GitHub Pages.
- Emailing support sends the email address, message, attachments, and other information the user chooses to provide to Google Gmail.
- Opening Google Maps or Waze sends selected addresses, coordinates, route stops, travel mode, and standard request data to the selected service.
- Opening a cited source, image credit, or GitHub Issue sends the selected URL and standard request data to that service.

MindSwipe does not attach local progress, saved cards, interests, or trip notes to external links.

## 4. Notifications and permissions

Reminders are optional and scheduled locally. Android may request notification permission. If the user chooses **Background exact**, Android may also request Alarms and reminders access. Flexible reminders do not request exact timing access.

The Android app declares internet, notification, and exact-alarm permissions. It does not declare precise, approximate, foreground, or background location permission.

## 5. Purposes, sharing, and retention

On-device data is used to provide requested app features. Support communications are used to answer requests, investigate issues, protect MindSwipe, and meet legal obligations. Support correspondence is normally deleted within 12 months after a matter closes unless security, dispute, or legal records reasonably require longer retention.

MindSwipe does not sell personal data or share local progress with advertisers or analytics providers. It has no advertising, marketing, analytics, fingerprinting, or automated-decision SDK.

Local data remains until the user resets it, clears browser/app storage, or uninstalls MindSwipe. **Profile - Settings - Reset local progress** removes the active local record and migration/recovery backups.

## 6. Rights, age, and security

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a portable copy of personal data processed by the operator, and to complain to a data protection authority. Most MindSwipe data exists only on the user's device. Support-record requests can be sent by email and may require reasonable identity verification.

MindSwipe is intended for people aged 16 and older and is not designed as a child-directed service.

MindSwipe limits collection, uses HTTPS for hosted pages, disables Android cleartext traffic and cloud backup, and keeps signing credentials outside the source repository. Users should not place passwords, payment information, medical information, or other highly sensitive information in trip notes or support messages.

## 7. Changes

Accounts, cloud sync, analytics, ads, payments, AI services, remote packs, crash reporting, or location would require this policy and store disclosures to be updated before release. Material changes receive a new effective date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
